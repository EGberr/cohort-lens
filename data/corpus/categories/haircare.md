# Haircare: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: shampoo, hair products, hair oil, conditioner. Sector: Beauty & Personal Care.*

## Executive summary

71% of Gen Z consumers bought haircare in the last twelve months, spending an average of €19 per month. The category is growing modestly (+6.2% spend vs the 2025 wave). The 'skinification' of hair is real: scalp serums and bond-repair treatments are the growth engines, while basic shampoo remains a price-led supermarket purchase.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 71%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €19
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €160
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +6.2%
- Buyers in panel: 4,241 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 79% of women bought versus 61% of men. Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 70%, 25-29: 72%). By market, penetration is highest in Germany (75%) and lowest in Spain (66%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€22 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 74%, compared with 68% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (34% of buyers), followed by In store (19%) and YouTube (14%). TikTok accounts for 37% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 34% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (8.6% vs 7.4%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (42%), then Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (23%) and TikTok Shop (15%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 18% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 13% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is quality & durability (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of creator recommendation (29%) and price / value for money (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 61% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are unclear results and products not suited to hair type.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

38% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (12 points above the panel average of 25%). 34% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (16 points above the panel average of 17%), and 41% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Scalp care**
- **Bond repair**
- **Curly-hair routines**

## Implications for marketers

Segment the shelf: protect volume on basics with value packs, and build premium, hair-type-specific treatment lines with clear before/after evidence.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 34% of buyers.
- Build the offer around quality & durability, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 42% of purchases.
