# Small Kitchen Appliances: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: air fryer, blender, coffee machine, kitchen gadgets. Sector: Home & Living.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 31% of the Gen Z panel purchased small kitchen appliances, spending an average of €17 per month. The category is growing modestly (+3.3% spend vs the 2025 wave). Air fryers and portable blenders are Gen Z's first appliances; recipe content sells the machine better than specs.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 31%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €17
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €62
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +3.3%
- Buyers in panel: 1,856 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 34% of women bought versus 27% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 24% among 16-19 year-olds to 37% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Spain (38%) and lowest in the US (27%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€20 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 39%, compared with 27% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (33% of buyers), followed by Search engines (20%) and YouTube (17%). TikTok accounts for 36% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 30% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (21% vs 16%).

The main purchase channel is Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (47%), then Physical store (31%) and Brand website / app (10%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 13% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 8.6% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (29%) and health benefits (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 65% of buyers, 4 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are counter space and price.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

21% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (5 points below the panel average of 25%). 21% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (4 points above the panel average of 17%), and 36% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Air fryer recipes**
- **Portable blenders**
- **Protein ice cream makers**

## Implications for marketers

Sell with recipes: content partnerships and in-app recipe libraries turn an appliance into a habit.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 33% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...), which carries 47% of purchases.
