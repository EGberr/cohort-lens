# Low & No Alcohol: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: alcohol-free drinks, non-alcoholic beer, mocktails, sober curious. Sector: Food & Beverage.*

## Executive summary

34% of Gen Z consumers bought low & no alcohol in the last twelve months, spending an average of €23 per month. The category is one of the fastest-growing categories in the panel (+18.2% spend vs the 2025 wave). 'Sober curious' is mainstream: Gen Z drinks less and more intentionally, and non-alcoholic options need to feel like an adult social choice, not a compromise.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 34%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €23
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €93
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +18.2%
- Buyers in panel: 2,038 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 37% of women bought versus 30% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 27% among 16-19 year-olds to 39% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the US (39%) and lowest in Spain (31%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€26 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 40%, compared with 30% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (24% of buyers), followed by Friends & family (24%) and In store (21%). TikTok accounts for 26% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 24% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (7.6% vs 7.2%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (69%), then Online marketplaces (12%) and Brand website / app (10%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is health benefits (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of community & belonging (30%) and novelty & trends (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 52% of buyers, 10 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price same as alcoholic drinks and taste.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

29% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 26%). 4.7% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (12 points below the panel average of 17%), and 35% usually repurchase the same brand (3 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among low & no alcohol buyers, the most common product type is Alcohol-free beer (44% of buyers). Bar mocktail over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (28% of its buyers), while Non-alcoholic spirit skews older (48% aged 25-29).

- **Alcohol-free beer**: 44% of category buyers, typical price €2, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Bar mocktail**: 38% of category buyers, typical price €9, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Non-alcoholic spirit**: 18% of category buyers, typical price €25, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Implications for marketers

Position NA drinks as premium social rituals (bars, events) and avoid 'healthy' framing that kills the fun.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 24% of buyers.
- Build the offer around health benefits, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 69% of purchases.
