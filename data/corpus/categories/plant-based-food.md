# Plant-based Food: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: vegan food, plant-based meat, oat milk, meat alternatives. Sector: Food & Beverage.*

## Executive summary

Plant-based Food reached 42% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €28 per month. The category is in decline (-2.8% spend vs the 2025 wave). Plant-based is shifting from meat imitation to 'whole-food' plant protein: alt-meat stalls while tofu, legumes and oat milk keep growing.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 42%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €28
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €142
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: -2.8%
- Buyers in panel: 2,544 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 50% of women bought versus 34% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 36% among 16-19 year-olds to 47% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (48%) and lowest in France (36%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€35 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 51%, compared with 38% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by In store (29% of buyers), followed by TikTok (23%) and Friends & family (20%). TikTok accounts for 27% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 22% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (6.4% vs 6.0%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (78%), then Online marketplaces (8.7%) and Subscription (6.3%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is health benefits (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of sustainability (28%) and price / value for money (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 66% of buyers, 4 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price premium vs meat and ultra-processed perception.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

56% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (30 points above the panel average of 26%). 9.7% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (7 points below the panel average of 17%), and 29% usually repurchase the same brand (9 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among plant-based food buyers, the most common product type is Oat milk (45% of buyers). Plant-based burger over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (28% of its buyers), while Tofu skews older (44% aged 25-29).

- **Oat milk**: 45% of category buyers, typical price €2, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Plant-based burger**: 28% of category buyers, typical price €5, interest cooling, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Tofu**: 27% of category buyers, typical price €3, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Implications for marketers

Pivot messaging from imitation to clean, whole-food credentials and close the price gap through private-label partnerships.

- Prioritise In store for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 29% of buyers.
- Build the offer around health benefits, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 78% of purchases.
