# Demi-fine Jewelry: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: jewellery, jewelry, earrings, gold vermeil. Sector: Fashion & Accessories.*

## Executive summary

35% of Gen Z consumers bought demi-fine jewelry in the last twelve months, spending an average of €26 per month. The category is growing solidly (+12.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). Gen Z is trading up from costume to demi-fine jewelry because it survives showers and gym sessions — durability is the new luxury signal.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 35%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €26
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €109
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +12.4%
- Buyers in panel: 2,121 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 48% of women bought versus 22% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 31% among 16-19 year-olds to 39% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Spain (41%) and lowest in the US (30%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€30 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 42%, compared with 32% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (38% of buyers), followed by Instagram (32%) and Friends & family (12%). TikTok accounts for 39% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 39% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (9.0% vs 5.6%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (29%), then TikTok Shop (20%) and Instagram Shopping (17%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 22% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 17% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of quality & durability (29%) and brand identity & values (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 55% of buyers, 6 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are tarnishing and trust in material claims.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

42% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (17 points above the panel average of 25%). 34% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (17 points above the panel average of 17%), and 32% usually repurchase the same brand (7 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Waterproof jewelry**
- **Stacking**
- **Permanent bracelets**

## Implications for marketers

Make material proof (waterproof, recycled gold, hypoallergenic) the hero claim and offer stackable, giftable entry pieces.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 38% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 29% of purchases.
