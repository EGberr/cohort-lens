# Demi-fine Jewelry: Gen Z Category Report 2026

*Also covers: jewellery, jewelry, earrings, gold vermeil. Sector: Fashion & Accessories.*

## Executive summary

37% of Gen Z consumers bought demi-fine jewelry in the last twelve months, spending an average of €25 per month. The category is growing solidly (+12.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). Gen Z is trading up from costume to demi-fine jewelry because it survives showers and gym sessions — durability is the new luxury signal.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 37%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €25
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €110
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +12.4%
- Buyers in panel: 2,201 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 50% of women bought versus 22% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 34% among 16-19 year-olds to 41% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the US (39%) and lowest in Spain (34%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€31 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 42%, compared with 34% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (38% of buyers), followed by Instagram (29%) and Friends & family (12%). TikTok accounts for 40% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 37% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (9.5% vs 8.6%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (30%), then TikTok Shop (19%) and Online marketplaces (18%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 22% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 16% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of quality & durability (29%) and brand identity & values (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 56% of buyers, 6 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are tarnishing and trust in material claims.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

41% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (15 points above the panel average of 26%). 36% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (19 points above the panel average of 17%), and 31% usually repurchase the same brand (8 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among demi-fine jewelry buyers, the most common product type is Gold vermeil hoops (38% of buyers). Stacking rings over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (30% of its buyers), while Gold vermeil hoops skews older (43% aged 25-29).

- **Gold vermeil hoops**: 38% of category buyers, typical price €45, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Layered necklace**: 35% of category buyers, typical price €55, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.

## Implications for marketers

Make material proof (waterproof, recycled gold, hypoallergenic) the hero claim and offer stackable, giftable entry pieces.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 38% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 30% of purchases.
