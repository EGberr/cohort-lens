# Used Cars: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: second-hand cars, pre-owned cars, first car, used car. Sector: Cars & Mobility.*

## Executive summary

11% of Gen Z consumers bought used cars in the last twelve months, spending an average of €222 per month. The category is growing modestly (+6.3% spend vs the 2025 wave). The first car is usually used, small and financed: Gen Z buyers research on marketplaces and YouTube, rely on family for the final check, and insurance often costs as much as the loan.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 11%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €222
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €292
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +6.3%
- Buyers in panel: 657 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards men: 13% of men bought versus 9.0% of women. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 6.2% among 16-19 year-olds to 17% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in France (14%) and lowest in Spain (6.6%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€287 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 23%, compared with 4.9% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (31% of buyers), followed by Search engines (27%) and YouTube (19%). TikTok accounts for 14% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 14% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (25% vs 31%).

The main purchase channel is Online marketplaces (51%), then Physical store (32%) and Brand website / app (17%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is price / value for money (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of quality & durability (32%) and convenience (16%). Price is the decisive factor for 75% of buyers, 14 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are insurance costs for young drivers and fear of hidden faults.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

21% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (5 points below the panel average of 26%). 4.1% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (13 points below the panel average of 17%), and 34% usually repurchase the same brand (5 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among used cars buyers, the most common product type is Used small hatchback (54% of buyers). Vintage / classic car over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (19% of its buyers), while Used small hatchback skews older (55% aged 25-29).

- **Used small hatchback**: 54% of category buyers, typical price €9,000, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Used hybrid**: 35% of category buyers, typical price €16,000, interest rising, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Vintage / classic car**: 12% of category buyers, typical price €12,000, interest cooling, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.

## Trends to watch

- **Online car marketplaces**
- **Certified pre-owned**
- **Young-driver insurance**

## Implications for marketers

Bundle warranty, inspection reports and young-driver insurance into the offer; transparency beats price cuts in a low-trust category.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 31% of buyers.
- Build the offer around price / value for money, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Online marketplaces, which carries 51% of purchases.
