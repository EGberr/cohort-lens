# Online Learning: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: language learning, online courses, Duolingo, skills courses. Sector: Money & Services.*

## Executive summary

33% of Gen Z consumers bought online learning in the last twelve months, spending an average of €15 per month. The category is growing modestly (+4.9% spend vs the 2025 wave). Gamified learning keeps Gen Z engaged, but paid conversion depends on career outcomes — certificates must be recognised by employers.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 33%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €15
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €58
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +4.9%
- Buyers in panel: 2,002 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 37% of women bought versus 30% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 28% among 16-19 year-olds to 38% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Italy (40%) and lowest in France (29%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€17 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 39%, compared with 30% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (24% of buyers), followed by YouTube (24%) and Search engines (22%). TikTok accounts for 27% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 22% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (24% vs 20%).

The main purchase channel is Subscription (72%), then Brand website / app (24%) and Online marketplaces (4.1%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (31%) and community & belonging (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 64% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are lack of motivation and doubts about certificate value.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

7.7% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (18 points below the panel average of 26%). 2.7% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (14 points below the panel average of 17%), and 38% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among online learning buyers, the most common product type is Language learning app (47% of buyers). Language learning app over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (28% of its buyers), while Coding course skews older (47% aged 25-29).

- **Language learning app**: 47% of category buyers, typical price €12/month, interest stable, mostly bought via Subscription.
- **Coding course**: 30% of category buyers, typical price €40, interest rising, mostly bought via Subscription.
- **AI tutor**: 24% of category buyers, typical price €15/month, interest rising, mostly bought via Subscription.

## Trends to watch

## Implications for marketers

Link courses to concrete career outcomes and employer partnerships; streaks and community keep learners engaged.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 24% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Subscription, which carries 72% of purchases.
