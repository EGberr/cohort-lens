# Fragrance: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: perfume, body mist, cologne, scent. Sector: Beauty & Personal Care.*

## Executive summary

Fragrance reached 54% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €28 per month. The category is growing solidly (+14.8% spend vs the 2025 wave). #PerfumeTok has turned fragrance into a wardrobe: Gen Z owns several scents for moods and occasions, which is why discovery sets and body mists grow faster than full-size bottles.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 54%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €28
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €181
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +14.8%
- Buyers in panel: 3,227 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 62% of women bought versus 45% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 49% among 16-19 year-olds to 58% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in France (57%) and lowest in Italy (50%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€34 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 62%, compared with 50% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (46% of buyers), followed by In store (17%) and Instagram (13%). TikTok accounts for 49% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 43% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (5.4% vs 5.5%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (38%), then Online marketplaces (20%) and TikTok Shop (16%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 16% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 15% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is brand identity & values (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of creator recommendation (29%) and novelty & trends (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 52% of buyers, 9 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are cannot smell before buying online and price of full bottles.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

24% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 26%). 39% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (22 points above the panel average of 17%), and 28% usually repurchase the same brand (11 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among fragrance buyers, the most common product type is Body mist (42% of buyers). Body mist over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (30% of its buyers), while Eau de parfum skews older (45% aged 25-29).

- **Body mist**: 42% of category buyers, typical price €14, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Eau de parfum**: 33% of category buyers, typical price €65, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Fragrance discovery set**: 25% of category buyers, typical price €30, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Trends to watch

- **Gourmand notes**
- **Fragrance wardrobes**

## Implications for marketers

Invest in sampling at scale (discovery kits, minis, in-store scent bars) and give each scent a story that can be explained in a 20-second video.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 46% of buyers.
- Build the offer around brand identity & values, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 38% of purchases.
