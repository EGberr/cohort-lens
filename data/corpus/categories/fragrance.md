# Fragrance: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: perfume, body mist, cologne, scent. Sector: Beauty & Personal Care.*

## Executive summary

Fragrance reached 53% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €28 per month. The category is growing solidly (+14.8% spend vs the 2025 wave). #PerfumeTok has turned fragrance into a wardrobe: Gen Z owns several scents for moods and occasions, which is why discovery sets and body mists grow faster than full-size bottles.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 53%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €28
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €177
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +14.8%
- Buyers in panel: 3,168 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 60% of women bought versus 45% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 48% among 16-19 year-olds to 57% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Germany (57%) and lowest in the US (48%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€34 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 59%, compared with 49% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (45% of buyers), followed by In store (18%) and Instagram (12%). TikTok accounts for 48% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 42% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (5.7% vs 5.6%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (36%), then Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (20%) and TikTok Shop (16%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 21% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 14% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is brand identity & values (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of creator recommendation (30%) and novelty & trends (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 52% of buyers, 9 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are cannot smell before buying online and price of full bottles.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

23% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 25%). 39% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (22 points above the panel average of 17%), and 29% usually repurchase the same brand (9 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Scent layering**
- **Gourmand notes**
- **Fragrance wardrobes**

## Implications for marketers

Invest in sampling at scale (discovery kits, minis, in-store scent bars) and give each scent a story that can be explained in a 20-second video.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 45% of buyers.
- Build the offer around brand identity & values, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 36% of purchases.
