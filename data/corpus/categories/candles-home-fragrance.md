# Candles & Home Fragrance: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: candles, diffusers, room spray, home fragrance. Sector: Home & Living.*

## Executive summary

Candles & Home Fragrance reached 38% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €13 per month. The category is growing modestly (+6.7% spend vs the 2025 wave). Home fragrance is an affordable mood purchase; seasonal scents and 'clean-burning' claims drive trial.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 38%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €13
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €57
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +6.7%
- Buyers in panel: 2,256 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 51% of women bought versus 23% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 32% among 16-19 year-olds to 42% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (40%) and lowest in the US (34%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€16 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 43%, compared with 34% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (31% of buyers), followed by In store (26%) and Instagram (18%). TikTok accounts for 36% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 27% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (8.5% vs 3.8%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (49%), then Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (20%) and TikTok Shop (13%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 17% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 12% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of novelty & trends (29%) and price / value for money (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 63% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are can't smell online and toxin concerns.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

35% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (10 points above the panel average of 25%). 27% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (10 points above the panel average of 17%), and 27% usually repurchase the same brand (11 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Seasonal scents**
- **Clean-burn wax**
- **Scent-scaping**

## Implications for marketers

Run seasonal drops and transparent ingredient claims; offer samplers to solve the online smell problem.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 31% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 49% of purchases.
