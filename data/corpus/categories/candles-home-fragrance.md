# Candles & Home Fragrance: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: candles, diffusers, room spray, home fragrance. Sector: Home & Living.*

## Executive summary

Candles & Home Fragrance reached 39% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €13 per month. The category is growing modestly (+6.7% spend vs the 2025 wave). Home fragrance is an affordable mood purchase; seasonal scents and 'clean-burning' claims drive trial.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 39%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €13
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €58
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +6.7%
- Buyers in panel: 2,315 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 53% of women bought versus 24% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 34% among 16-19 year-olds to 43% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Spain (41%) and lowest in Germany (33%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€16 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 44%, compared with 35% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (31% of buyers), followed by In store (28%) and Instagram (17%). TikTok accounts for 37% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 28% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (6.3% vs 6.5%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (47%), then Online marketplaces (21%) and TikTok Shop (14%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 16% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 12% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of novelty & trends (28%) and price / value for money (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 64% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are can't smell online and toxin concerns.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

36% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (10 points above the panel average of 26%). 29% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (12 points above the panel average of 17%), and 29% usually repurchase the same brand (10 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among candles & home fragrance buyers, the most common product type is Scented candle (48% of buyers). Room spray over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (30% of its buyers), while Reed diffuser skews older (47% aged 25-29).

- **Scented candle**: 48% of category buyers, typical price €22, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Reed diffuser**: 27% of category buyers, typical price €28, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Room spray**: 25% of category buyers, typical price €12, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Trends to watch

- **Seasonal scents**
- **Clean-burn wax**
- **Scent-scaping**

## Implications for marketers

Run seasonal drops and transparent ingredient claims; offer samplers to solve the online smell problem.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 31% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 47% of purchases.
