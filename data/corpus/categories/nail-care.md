# Nail Care: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: nails, nail polish, press-on nails, gel nails. Sector: Beauty & Personal Care.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 39% of the Gen Z panel purchased nail care, spending an average of €15 per month. The category is growing solidly (+7.9% spend vs the 2025 wave). Press-on nails have democratised salon looks: Gen Z buys nail art as a weekly accessory rather than a monthly service, driven by seasonal designs that sell out on TikTok Shop.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 39%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €15
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €69
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +7.9%
- Buyers in panel: 2,328 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 55% of women bought versus 21% of men. Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 40%, 25-29: 38%). By market, penetration is highest in Spain (44%) and lowest in Germany (34%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€18 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 42%, compared with 36% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (48% of buyers), followed by Instagram (24%) and Friends & family (10%). TikTok accounts for 49% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 47% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (4.3% vs 3.5%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (31%), then TikTok Shop (27%) and Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (22%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 30% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 25% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (30%) and novelty & trends (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 74% of buyers, 13 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are time it takes and salon prices.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

20% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (6 points below the panel average of 25%). 36% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (19 points above the panel average of 17%), and 21% usually repurchase the same brand (17 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Press-ons**
- **Chrome nails**
- **At-home gel kits**

## Implications for marketers

Run a fast, drop-based design calendar and collaborate with nail artists on limited sets; reusable press-ons are a credible sustainability story.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 48% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 31% of purchases.
