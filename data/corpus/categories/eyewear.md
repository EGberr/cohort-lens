# Eyewear: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: glasses, sunglasses, frames, contact lenses. Sector: Fashion & Accessories.*

## Executive summary

33% of Gen Z consumers bought eyewear in the last twelve months, spending an average of €20 per month. The category is growing modestly (+4.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). Glasses have become a fashion accessory first and a medical device second; virtual try-on is now table stakes for online sales.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 33%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €20
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €79
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +4.4%
- Buyers in panel: 1,971 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 35% of women bought versus 31% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 28% among 16-19 year-olds to 38% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Italy (37%) and lowest in the US (31%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€24 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 40%, compared with 28% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by In store (25% of buyers), followed by TikTok (23%) and Instagram (22%). TikTok accounts for 24% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 22% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (17% vs 16%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (45%), then Brand website / app (25%) and Online marketplaces (20%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (29%) and quality & durability (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 62% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are need for prescriptions and trying on online.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

21% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (5 points below the panel average of 26%). 21% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (4 points above the panel average of 17%), and 34% usually repurchase the same brand (5 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among eyewear buyers, the most common product type is Sunglasses (44% of buyers). Sunglasses over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (30% of its buyers), while Monthly contact lenses skews older (48% aged 25-29).

- **Sunglasses**: 44% of category buyers, typical price €60, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Statement frames**: 34% of category buyers, typical price €120, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Monthly contact lenses**: 22% of category buyers, typical price €25, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Trends to watch

- **Statement frames**
- **Virtual try-on**
- **Blue-light skepticism**

## Implications for marketers

Invest in virtual try-on and home try-on kits; drop seasonal frame colours like a fashion brand.

- Prioritise In store for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 25% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 45% of purchases.
