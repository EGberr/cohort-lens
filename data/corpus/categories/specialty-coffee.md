# Specialty Coffee: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: coffee, iced coffee, matcha, coffee shops. Sector: Food & Beverage.*

## Executive summary

66% of Gen Z consumers bought specialty coffee in the last twelve months, spending an average of €34 per month. The category is growing solidly (+7.5% spend vs the 2025 wave). Coffee shops function as Gen Z's 'third place' and status symbol; iced and matcha drinks out-sell hot coffee in this cohort, and the cup is part of the content.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 66%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €34
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €267
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +7.5%
- Buyers in panel: 3,970 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 72% of women bought versus 60% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 62% among 16-19 year-olds to 71% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (72%) and lowest in Germany (62%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€42 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 71%, compared with 62% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (29% of buyers), followed by Friends & family (23%) and Instagram (22%). TikTok accounts for 33% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 27% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (6.1% vs 6.3%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (65%), then Brand website / app (12%) and Subscription (11%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of quality & durability (30%) and community & belonging (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 58% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are cost of daily purchases and sugar in specialty drinks.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

39% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (13 points above the panel average of 26%). 8.4% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (8 points below the panel average of 17%), and 45% usually repurchase the same brand (7 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among specialty coffee buyers, the most common product type is Iced latte (43% of buyers). Matcha latte over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (30% of its buyers), while Specialty coffee beans skews older (48% aged 25-29).

- **Iced latte**: 43% of category buyers, typical price €5, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Matcha latte**: 36% of category buyers, typical price €5, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Specialty coffee beans**: 21% of category buyers, typical price €14, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Trends to watch

- **Matcha boom**
- **Iced-everything**
- **Café as third place**

## Implications for marketers

Design for the photo (cups, colours, seasonal menus) and use loyalty apps to convert a daily habit into a programme.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 29% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 65% of purchases.
