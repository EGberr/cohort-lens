# Smartphones: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: phones, mobile phones, iPhone, Android phones. Sector: Tech & Electronics.*

## Executive summary

42% of Gen Z consumers bought smartphones in the last twelve months, spending an average of €44 per month. The category is broadly flat (+0.8% spend vs the 2025 wave). The phone is the most loyal purchase in the panel: ecosystem lock-in is strong, but refurbished phones are now a mainstream way to stay on a flagship brand for less.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 42%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €44
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €220
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +0.8%
- Buyers in panel: 2,507 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

Purchase is balanced by gender (women 41%, men 43%). It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 39% among 16-19 year-olds to 45% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (46%) and lowest in Spain (38%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€53 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 50%, compared with 35% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by YouTube (30% of buyers), followed by Search engines (20%) and TikTok (18%). TikTok accounts for 20% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 17% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (21% vs 17%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (30%), then Physical store (28%) and Online marketplaces (22%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is brand identity & values (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of performance & specs (29%) and quality & durability (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 54% of buyers, 7 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price of flagship models and carrier contracts.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

29% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 26%). 6.4% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (10 points below the panel average of 17%), and 72% usually repurchase the same brand (33 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among smartphones buyers, the most common product type is Refurbished smartphone (36% of buyers). Refurbished smartphone over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (28% of its buyers), while Flagship smartphone skews older (41% aged 25-29).

- **Refurbished smartphone**: 36% of category buyers, typical price €420, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Flagship smartphone**: 34% of category buyers, typical price €950, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Mid-range smartphone**: 31% of category buyers, typical price €350, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Trends to watch

- **Refurbished flagships**
- **Camera-first purchases**
- **Longer upgrade cycles**

## Implications for marketers

Promote certified refurbished and trade-in as part of the main range, not an outlet; camera and battery are the decisive specs.

- Prioritise YouTube for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 30% of buyers.
- Build the offer around brand identity & values, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 30% of purchases.
