# Collectibles & Trading Cards: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: trading cards, blind boxes, collectible toys, figures. Sector: Entertainment & Experiences.*

## Executive summary

24% of Gen Z consumers bought collectibles & trading cards in the last twelve months, spending an average of €23 per month. The category is one of the fastest-growing categories in the panel (+19.8% spend vs the 2025 wave). Blind boxes and trading cards are the fastest-growing hobby spend: unboxing is the content, and the thrill of rarity drives repeat purchase.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 24%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €23
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €67
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +19.8%
- Buyers in panel: 1,440 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 26% of women bought versus 22% of men. Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 24%, 25-29: 23%). By market, penetration is highest in Spain (27%) and lowest in the US (18%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€29 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 28%, compared with 22% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (42% of buyers), followed by YouTube (20%) and Friends & family (17%). TikTok accounts for 44% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 39% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (5.1% vs 3.4%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (29%), then Online marketplaces (28%) and Resale apps (Vinted, Depop...) (22%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 14% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 11% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is exclusivity & drops (43% of buyers cite it first), ahead of community & belonging (28%) and novelty & trends (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 45% of buyers, 17 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are speculation and scalping and spending addiction concerns.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

8.3% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (18 points below the panel average of 26%). 16% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (in line with the panel average of 17%), and 50% usually repurchase the same brand (11 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among collectibles & trading cards buyers, the most common product type is Blind-box figure (39% of buyers). Trading card booster over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (31% of its buyers), while Vinyl figure skews older (43% aged 25-29).

- **Blind-box figure**: 39% of category buyers, typical price €15, interest rising, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Trading card booster**: 36% of category buyers, typical price €6, interest rising, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Vinyl figure**: 24% of category buyers, typical price €18, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.

## Implications for marketers

Design for the unboxing moment and community trading; build safeguards against scalping to keep the hobby healthy.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 42% of buyers.
- Build the offer around exclusivity & drops, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 29% of purchases.
