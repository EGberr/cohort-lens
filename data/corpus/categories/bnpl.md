# Buy Now, Pay Later: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: BNPL, Klarna, pay in 3, instalment payments. Sector: Money & Services.*

## Executive summary

36% of Gen Z consumers bought buy now, pay later in the last twelve months, spending an average of €56 per month. The category is growing solidly (+7.2% spend vs the 2025 wave). BNPL is used for budgeting more than for luxury; a significant minority report stress from stacking several plans.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 36%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €56
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €243
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +7.2%
- Buyers in panel: 2,149 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 38% of women bought versus 34% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 29% among 16-19 year-olds to 41% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in France (40%) and lowest in Italy (29%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€68 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 45%, compared with 30% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (30% of buyers), followed by Friends & family (25%) and Instagram (18%). TikTok accounts for 35% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 27% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (16% vs 15%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (55%), then Online marketplaces (39%) and TikTok Shop (5.6%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (29%) and brand identity & values (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 75% of buyers, 13 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are debt worries and late fees.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

4.7% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (21 points below the panel average of 26%). 1.6% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (15 points below the panel average of 17%), and 41% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among buy now, pay later buyers, the most common product type is Pay in 3 (53% of buyers). Pay in 30 days over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (26% of its buyers), while Long financing plan skews older (46% aged 25-29).

- **Pay in 3**: 53% of category buyers, typical price €120, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Pay in 30 days**: 32% of category buyers, typical price €60, interest cooling, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Long financing plan**: 15% of category buyers, typical price €600, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.

## Implications for marketers

Integrate responsible-spending tools and transparent schedules — trust is the long-term growth lever.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 30% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 55% of purchases.
