# Buy Now, Pay Later: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: BNPL, Klarna, pay in 3, instalment payments. Sector: Money & Services.*

## Executive summary

36% of Gen Z consumers bought buy now, pay later in the last twelve months, spending an average of €55 per month. The category is growing solidly (+7.2% spend vs the 2025 wave). BNPL is used for budgeting more than for luxury; a significant minority report stress from stacking several plans.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 36%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €55
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €241
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +7.2%
- Buyers in panel: 2,188 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 40% of women bought versus 33% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 29% among 16-19 year-olds to 43% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Italy (42%) and lowest in France (34%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€71 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 46%, compared with 30% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (28% of buyers), followed by TikTok (26%) and Instagram (17%). TikTok accounts for 30% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 24% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (15% vs 14%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (54%), then Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (39%) and TikTok Shop (6.4%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 8.2% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 6.2% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (29%) and brand identity & values (18%). Price is the decisive factor for 75% of buyers, 14 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are debt worries and late fees.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

5.1% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (20 points below the panel average of 25%). 2.1% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (15 points below the panel average of 17%), and 41% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Bnpl regulation**
- **Loud budgeting**

## Implications for marketers

Integrate responsible-spending tools and transparent schedules — trust is the long-term growth lever.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 28% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 54% of purchases.
