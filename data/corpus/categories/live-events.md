# Live Events & Concerts: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: concerts, festivals, live music, tickets. Sector: Entertainment & Media.*

## Executive summary

Live Events & Concerts reached 48% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €38 per month. The category is growing solidly (+13.6% spend vs the 2025 wave). Experiences beat things: Gen Z prioritises concerts even under budget pressure, saving and using BNPL to afford headline tours.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 48%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €38
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €220
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +13.6%
- Buyers in panel: 2,867 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 51% of women bought versus 44% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 43% among 16-19 year-olds to 53% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in France (52%) and lowest in Italy (40%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€46 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 57%, compared with 43% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (32% of buyers), followed by TikTok (26%) and Instagram (23%). TikTok accounts for 30% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 24% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (9.4% vs 6.3%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (56%), then Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (30%) and Resale apps (Vinted, Depop...) (14%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is community & belonging (43% of buyers cite it first), ahead of exclusivity & drops (28%) and brand identity & values (18%). Price is the decisive factor for 50% of buyers, 11 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are ticket prices and fees and resale scalping.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

18% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (8 points below the panel average of 25%). 2.9% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (14 points below the panel average of 17%), and 30% usually repurchase the same brand (9 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Eras-style tours**
- **Bnpl for tickets**
- **Fan communities**

## Implications for marketers

Package experiences (merch + ticket + content moments) and fight scalping to protect trust with fans.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 32% of buyers.
- Build the offer around community & belonging, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 56% of purchases.
