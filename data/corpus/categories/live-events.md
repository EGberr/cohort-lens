# Live Events & Concerts: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: concerts, festivals, live music, tickets. Sector: Entertainment & Experiences.*

## Executive summary

Live Events & Concerts reached 50% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €37 per month. The category is growing solidly (+13.6% spend vs the 2025 wave). Experiences beat things: Gen Z prioritises concerts even under budget pressure, saving and using BNPL to afford headline tours.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 50%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €37
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €224
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +13.6%
- Buyers in panel: 3,009 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 53% of women bought versus 47% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 45% among 16-19 year-olds to 54% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Spain (54%) and lowest in the US (43%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€45 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 58%, compared with 44% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (32% of buyers), followed by TikTok (25%) and Instagram (25%). TikTok accounts for 28% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 24% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (8.5% vs 7.9%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (53%), then Online marketplaces (30%) and Resale apps (Vinted, Depop...) (17%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is community & belonging (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of exclusivity & drops (29%) and brand identity & values (18%). Price is the decisive factor for 49% of buyers, 13 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are ticket prices and fees and resale scalping.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

17% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (9 points below the panel average of 26%). 3.3% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (14 points below the panel average of 17%), and 29% usually repurchase the same brand (9 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among live events & concerts buyers, the most common product type is Stadium concert ticket (43% of buyers). Stadium concert ticket over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (26% of its buyers), while Festival pass skews older (40% aged 25-29).

- **Stadium concert ticket**: 43% of category buyers, typical price €95, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Club night**: 30% of category buyers, typical price €20, interest cooling, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Festival pass**: 27% of category buyers, typical price €220, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.

## Trends to watch

## Implications for marketers

Package experiences (merch + ticket + content moments) and fight scalping to protect trust with fans.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 32% of buyers.
- Build the offer around community & belonging, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 53% of purchases.
