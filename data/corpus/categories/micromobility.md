# Micromobility: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: e-scooters, e-bikes, bike sharing, scooters. Sector: Cars & Mobility.*

## Executive summary

Micromobility reached 27% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €16 per month. The category is growing solidly (+8.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). Shared e-bikes are replacing short ride-hail and transit trips; regulation (e-scooter bans) reshapes the market city by city.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 27%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €16
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €51
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +8.4%
- Buyers in panel: 1,615 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards men: 34% of men bought versus 21% of women. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 23% among 16-19 year-olds to 30% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the US (32%) and lowest in Spain (24%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€18 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 32%, compared with 25% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (27% of buyers), followed by In store (26%) and TikTok (19%). TikTok accounts for 25% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 15% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (15% vs 12%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (59%), then Subscription (22%) and Online marketplaces (10%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (29%) and sustainability (18%). Price is the decisive factor for 69% of buyers, 8 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are safety and regulation and bans.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

42% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (16 points above the panel average of 26%). 4.5% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (12 points below the panel average of 17%), and 33% usually repurchase the same brand (6 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among micromobility buyers, the most common product type is Shared e-bike (48% of buyers). E-scooter over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (31% of its buyers), while Personal e-bike skews older (47% aged 25-29).

- **Shared e-bike**: 48% of category buyers, typical price €15, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **E-scooter**: 32% of category buyers, typical price €450, interest cooling, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Personal e-bike**: 20% of category buyers, typical price €1,200, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.

## Trends to watch

- **E-bike passes**
- **Scooter bans**
- **15-minute cities**

## Implications for marketers

Offer monthly passes and lead on safety; partner with universities and employers.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 27% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 59% of purchases.
