# Music Streaming: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: Spotify, music subscriptions, streaming music. Sector: Entertainment & Media.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 73% of the Gen Z panel purchased music streaming, spending an average of €9 per month. The category is growing modestly (+2.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). Music streaming is near-universal and sticky; TikTok shapes what is streamed, and student and family plans hide true price sensitivity.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 73%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €9
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €82
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +2.4%
- Buyers in panel: 4,377 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

Purchase is balanced by gender (women 74%, men 72%). Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 72%, 25-29: 74%). By market, penetration is highest in Italy (78%) and lowest in Spain (70%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€11 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 77%, compared with 70% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (33% of buyers), followed by TikTok (30%) and YouTube (14%). TikTok accounts for 33% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 27% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (9.5% vs 6.8%).

The main purchase channel is Subscription (85%), then Brand website / app (15%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of community & belonging (30%) and price / value for money (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 63% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price increases and account sharing restrictions.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

6.4% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (19 points below the panel average of 25%). 2.1% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (15 points below the panel average of 17%), and 76% usually repurchase the same brand (37 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Tiktok-to-chart pipeline**
- **Annual recaps**
- **Student plans**

## Implications for marketers

Use social features (shared playlists, wrapped-style recaps) to defend retention when prices increase.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 33% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Subscription, which carries 85% of purchases.
