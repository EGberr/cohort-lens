# Music Streaming: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: Spotify, music subscriptions, streaming music. Sector: Entertainment & Experiences.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 74% of the Gen Z panel purchased music streaming, spending an average of €9 per month. The category is growing modestly (+2.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). Music streaming is near-universal and sticky; TikTok shapes what is streamed, and student and family plans hide true price sensitivity.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 74%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €9
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €81
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +2.4%
- Buyers in panel: 4,415 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

Purchase is balanced by gender (women 75%, men 72%). Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 72%, 25-29: 75%). By market, penetration is highest in the US (76%) and lowest in Spain (71%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€11 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 78%, compared with 71% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (33% of buyers), followed by TikTok (29%) and YouTube (14%). TikTok accounts for 31% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 27% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (8.8% vs 7.8%).

The main purchase channel is Subscription (85%), then Brand website / app (15%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is convenience (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of community & belonging (29%) and price / value for money (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 63% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price increases and account sharing restrictions.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

6.1% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (20 points below the panel average of 26%). 1.7% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (15 points below the panel average of 17%), and 76% usually repurchase the same brand (37 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among music streaming buyers, the most common product type is Student plan (42% of buyers). Student plan over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (34% of its buyers), while Individual plan skews older (42% aged 25-29).

- **Student plan**: 42% of category buyers, typical price €6/month, interest stable, mostly bought via Subscription.
- **Individual plan**: 37% of category buyers, typical price €11/month, interest stable, mostly bought via Subscription.
- **Family / duo plan**: 21% of category buyers, typical price €17/month, interest rising, mostly bought via Subscription.

## Trends to watch

- **Tiktok-to-chart pipeline**
- **Annual recaps**
- **Student plans**

## Implications for marketers

Use social features (shared playlists, wrapped-style recaps) to defend retention when prices increase.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 33% of buyers.
- Build the offer around convenience, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Subscription, which carries 85% of purchases.
