# Electric Vehicles: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: EV, electric cars, Tesla, electric car. Sector: Cars & Mobility.*

## Executive summary

6.4% of Gen Z consumers bought electric vehicles in the last twelve months, spending an average of €370 per month. The category is one of the fastest-growing categories in the panel (+24.5% spend vs the 2025 wave). Few Gen Z consumers can afford a new car, but those who buy one increasingly choose electric: leasing and small city EVs are the entry point, and YouTube range tests outrank dealer advice.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 6.4%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €370
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €283
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +24.5%
- Buyers in panel: 382 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

Purchase is balanced by gender (women 4.9%, men 8.2%). It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 3.0% among 16-19 year-olds to 9.9% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (7.9%) and lowest in Germany (4.4%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€495 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 18%, compared with 1.4% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by YouTube (36% of buyers), followed by Search engines (25%) and Friends & family (17%). TikTok accounts for 9.6% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 14% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (25% vs 25%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (48%), then Physical store (39%) and Subscription (13%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is sustainability (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of performance & specs (30%) and price / value for money (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 57% of buyers, 5 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are purchase price and charging access for renters.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

55% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (29 points above the panel average of 26%). 1.8% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (15 points below the panel average of 17%), and 38% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among electric vehicles buyers, the most common product type is Compact city EV (44% of buyers). Electric crossover over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (15% of its buyers), while Electric crossover skews older (57% aged 25-29).

- **Compact city EV**: 44% of category buyers, typical price €24,000, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Social leasing EV**: 32% of category buyers, typical price €100/month, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Electric crossover**: 24% of category buyers, typical price €42,000, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.

## Trends to watch

## Implications for marketers

Lead with total cost of ownership and leasing offers rather than sticker price; partner with EV review creators and solve home-charging for renters.

- Prioritise YouTube for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 36% of buyers.
- Build the offer around sustainability, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 48% of purchases.
