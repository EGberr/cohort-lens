# Houseplants: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: plants, indoor plants, plant care. Sector: Home & Living.*

## Executive summary

Houseplants reached 23% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €12 per month. The category is in decline (-3.5% spend vs the 2025 wave). The pandemic plant boom has normalised: buyers now prefer low-maintenance plants and care tools over rare collector plants.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 23%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €12
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €32
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: -3.5%
- Buyers in panel: 1,398 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 29% of women bought versus 16% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 20% among 16-19 year-olds to 28% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (28%) and lowest in the US (19%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€14 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 27%, compared with 23% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by In store (31% of buyers), followed by Instagram (25%) and TikTok (21%). TikTok accounts for 23% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 20% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (4.9% vs 3.9%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (65%), then Online marketplaces (14%) and Brand website / app (10%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of health benefits (30%) and community & belonging (18%). Price is the decisive factor for 59% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are plants dying and space.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

39% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (13 points above the panel average of 26%). 2.9% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (14 points below the panel average of 17%), and 25% usually repurchase the same brand (14 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among houseplants buyers, the most common product type is Pothos (39% of buyers). Succulent set over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (28% of its buyers), while Pothos skews older (43% aged 25-29).

- **Pothos**: 39% of category buyers, typical price €15, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Succulent set**: 33% of category buyers, typical price €18, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Monstera**: 28% of category buyers, typical price €35, interest cooling, mostly bought via Physical store.

## Implications for marketers

Sell 'hard-to-kill' and plant-care education; rare plants are a niche, not the mainstream.

- Prioritise In store for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 31% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
