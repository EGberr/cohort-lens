# Books: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: BookTok, novels, e-books, reading. Sector: Entertainment & Media.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 46% of the Gen Z panel purchased books, spending an average of €15 per month. The category is growing solidly (+8.3% spend vs the 2025 wave). BookTok has made physical books a collectible: special editions with sprayed edges sell out, and romance and fantasy dominate.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 46%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €15
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €83
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +8.3%
- Buyers in panel: 2,789 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 62% of women bought versus 30% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 43% among 16-19 year-olds to 48% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in France (49%) and lowest in the UK (43%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€18 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 52%, compared with 44% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (44% of buyers), followed by Friends & family (19%) and In store (15%). TikTok accounts for 46% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 42% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (4.5% vs 3.3%).

The main purchase channel is Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (39%), then Physical store (39%) and Resale apps (Vinted, Depop...) (12%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is creator recommendation (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of community & belonging (31%) and aesthetics & self-expression (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 55% of buyers, 6 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price of new hardbacks and time to read.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

19% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (6 points below the panel average of 25%). 3.5% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (14 points below the panel average of 17%), and 41% usually repurchase the same brand (in line with the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Special editions**
- **Romantasy**
- **Book clubs**

## Implications for marketers

Invest in special editions and creator early-reads; physical bookstores win with BookTok tables.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 44% of buyers.
- Build the offer around creator recommendation, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...), which carries 39% of purchases.
