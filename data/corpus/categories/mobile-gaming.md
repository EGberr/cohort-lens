# Mobile Gaming (In-app Purchases): Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: mobile games, in-app purchases, gaming apps, battle pass. Sector: Entertainment & Media.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 46% of the Gen Z panel purchased mobile gaming (in-app purchases), spending an average of €17 per month. The category is growing modestly (+5.3% spend vs the 2025 wave). Spending concentrates on cosmetic items and battle passes, purchased to express identity within friend groups rather than to win.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 46%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €17
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €92
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +5.3%
- Buyers in panel: 2,782 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards men: 55% of men bought versus 39% of women. It is driven by younger Gen Z: 49% of 16-19 year-olds bought, against 44% of 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the UK (51%) and lowest in Italy (41%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€20 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 50%, compared with 44% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (29% of buyers), followed by TikTok (28%) and YouTube (23%). TikTok accounts for 31% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 27% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (7.7% vs 5.7%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (78%), then Subscription (22%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is community & belonging (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of novelty & trends (29%) and exclusivity & drops (18%). Price is the decisive factor for 58% of buyers, 3 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are pay-to-win mechanics and spending regret.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

4.3% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (21 points below the panel average of 25%). 1.6% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (16 points below the panel average of 17%), and 52% usually repurchase the same brand (13 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Cosmetic skins**
- **Battle passes**
- **Brand collabs in games**

## Implications for marketers

Monetise self-expression (skins, collaborations) rather than power; social features drive both acquisition and spend.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 29% of buyers.
- Build the offer around community & belonging, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 78% of purchases.
