# Mobile Gaming (In-app Purchases): Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: mobile games, in-app purchases, gaming apps, battle pass. Sector: Entertainment & Experiences.*

## Executive summary

Over the past year, 45% of the Gen Z panel purchased mobile gaming (in-app purchases), spending an average of €17 per month. The category is growing modestly (+5.3% spend vs the 2025 wave). Spending concentrates on cosmetic items and battle passes, purchased to express identity within friend groups rather than to win.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 45%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €17
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €89
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +5.3%
- Buyers in panel: 2,684 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards men: 53% of men bought versus 37% of women. Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 45%, 25-29: 43%). By market, penetration is highest in Italy (49%) and lowest in France (39%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€19 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 50%, compared with 41% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by Friends & family (29% of buyers), followed by TikTok (26%) and YouTube (23%). TikTok accounts for 29% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 25% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (6.1% vs 5.9%).

The main purchase channel is Brand website / app (79%), then Subscription (21%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is community & belonging (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of novelty & trends (29%) and exclusivity & drops (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 58% of buyers, 3 points below the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are pay-to-win mechanics and spending regret.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

3.9% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (22 points below the panel average of 26%). 1.8% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (15 points below the panel average of 17%), and 52% usually repurchase the same brand (13 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among mobile gaming (in-app purchases) buyers, the most common product type is Battle pass (42% of buyers). Cosmetic skin over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (31% of its buyers), while Game subscription skews older (38% aged 25-29).

- **Battle pass**: 42% of category buyers, typical price €10, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Cosmetic skin**: 35% of category buyers, typical price €8, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Game subscription**: 24% of category buyers, typical price €6/month, interest stable, mostly bought via Brand website / app.

## Trends to watch

- **Cosmetic skins**
- **Battle passes**
- **Brand collabs in games**

## Implications for marketers

Monetise self-expression (skins, collaborations) rather than power; social features drive both acquisition and spend.

- Prioritise Friends & family for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 29% of buyers.
- Build the offer around community & belonging, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Brand website / app, which carries 79% of purchases.
