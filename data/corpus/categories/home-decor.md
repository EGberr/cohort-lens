# Home Decor: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: decor, room decor, interior accessories, posters. Sector: Home & Living.*

## Executive summary

Home Decor reached 47% of Gen Z consumers over the last twelve months, spending an average of €22 per month. The category is growing modestly (+4.2% spend vs the 2025 wave). Renters decorate for self-expression with removable, affordable pieces; 'aesthetic' room tours set the trends.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 47%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €22
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €124
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +4.2%
- Buyers in panel: 2,817 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 58% of women bought versus 35% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 41% among 16-19 year-olds to 51% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in the US (52%) and lowest in the UK (41%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€26 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 52%, compared with 42% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (34% of buyers), followed by Instagram (22%) and In store (17%). TikTok accounts for 36% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 34% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (12% vs 9.2%).

The main purchase channel is Physical store (32%), then Online marketplaces (31%) and TikTok Shop (14%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 16% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 14% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is aesthetics & self-expression (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (29%) and brand identity & values (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 67% of buyers, 5 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are renting restrictions and small spaces.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

25% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 26%). 29% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (12 points above the panel average of 17%), and 18% usually repurchase the same brand (20 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among home decor buyers, the most common product type is Poster print (40% of buyers). Poster print over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (29% of its buyers), while Statement mirror skews older (43% aged 25-29).

- **Poster print**: 40% of category buyers, typical price €20, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Throw blanket**: 33% of category buyers, typical price €35, interest stable, mostly bought via Physical store.
- **Statement mirror**: 27% of category buyers, typical price €70, interest rising, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.

## Trends to watch

- **Renter-friendly decor**
- **Maximalism**
- **Room tours**

## Implications for marketers

Make products renter-proof (no drilling) and photographable; collaborate with room-tour creators.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 34% of buyers.
- Build the offer around aesthetics & self-expression, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Physical store, which carries 32% of purchases.
