# Gaming Hardware: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: consoles, gaming PC, controllers, gaming peripherals. Sector: Tech & Electronics.*

## Executive summary

37% of Gen Z consumers bought gaming hardware in the last twelve months, spending an average of €42 per month. The category is broadly flat (-1.5% spend vs the 2025 wave). Hardware spend is cyclical and soft between console generations; peripherals and handheld PCs carry growth, and YouTube reviews are decisive.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 37%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €42
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €187
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: -1.5%
- Buyers in panel: 2,237 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards men: 52% of men bought versus 24% of women. Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 38%, 25-29: 36%). By market, penetration is highest in Germany (42%) and lowest in Spain (31%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€51 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 42%, compared with 34% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by YouTube (35% of buyers), followed by TikTok (19%) and Friends & family (18%). TikTok accounts for 22% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 18% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (17% vs 14%).

The main purchase channel is Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (39%), then Physical store (27%) and Brand website / app (19%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is performance & specs (40% of buyers cite it first), ahead of community & belonging (30%) and quality & durability (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 59% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are price of new consoles and game prices.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

12% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (13 points below the panel average of 25%). 8.2% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (9 points below the panel average of 17%), and 60% usually repurchase the same brand (21 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Implications for marketers

Win the review ecosystem (YouTube, Twitch) and bundle with game passes to lower the upfront price.

- Prioritise YouTube for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 35% of buyers.
- Build the offer around performance & specs, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...), which carries 39% of purchases.
