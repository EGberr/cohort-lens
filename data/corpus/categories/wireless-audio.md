# Wireless Audio: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: headphones, earbuds, earphones, speakers. Sector: Tech & Electronics.*

## Executive summary

48% of Gen Z consumers bought wireless audio in the last twelve months, spending an average of €15 per month. The category is growing modestly (+3.9% spend vs the 2025 wave). Over-ear headphones have become a fashion statement again, while earbuds are bought as near-disposable utility items on marketplaces.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 48%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €15
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €89
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +3.9%
- Buyers in panel: 2,894 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

Purchase is balanced by gender (women 47%, men 50%). Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 49%, 25-29: 49%). By market, penetration is highest in Italy (52%) and lowest in Spain (44%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€18 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 54%, compared with 45% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (28% of buyers), followed by YouTube (26%) and Search engines (16%). TikTok accounts for 30% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 26% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (17% vs 14%).

The main purchase channel is Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (37%), then Physical store (27%) and Brand website / app (17%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 13% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 9.6% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is performance & specs (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of aesthetics & self-expression (29%) and brand identity & values (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 60% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are fragility / losing earbuds and price.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

20% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (5 points below the panel average of 25%). 30% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (13 points above the panel average of 17%), and 48% usually repurchase the same brand (9 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Headphones as accessory**
- **Noise cancelling**
- **Open-ear buds**

## Implications for marketers

Market over-ears as fashion (colourways, collaborations) and earbuds on battery life and price.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 28% of buyers.
- Build the offer around performance & specs, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...), which carries 37% of purchases.
