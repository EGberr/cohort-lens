# Wireless Audio: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: headphones, earbuds, earphones, speakers. Sector: Tech & Electronics.*

## Executive summary

47% of Gen Z consumers bought wireless audio in the last twelve months, spending an average of €15 per month. The category is growing modestly (+3.9% spend vs the 2025 wave). Over-ear headphones have become a fashion statement again, while earbuds are bought as near-disposable utility items on marketplaces.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 47%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €15
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €88
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +3.9%
- Buyers in panel: 2,846 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

Purchase is balanced by gender (women 47%, men 47%). Penetration is similar across age bands (16-19: 48%, 25-29: 47%). By market, penetration is highest in Italy (51%) and lowest in the US (44%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€18 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 52%, compared with 45% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (29% of buyers), followed by YouTube (26%) and Search engines (16%). TikTok accounts for 30% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 28% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (16% vs 16%).

The main purchase channel is Online marketplaces (36%), then Physical store (28%) and Brand website / app (18%). Social commerce through TikTok Shop reaches 13% of purchases among the youngest buyers, against 9.2% among 25-29 year-olds.

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is performance & specs (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of aesthetics & self-expression (29%) and brand identity & values (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 61% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are fragility / losing earbuds and price.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

21% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (5 points below the panel average of 26%). 31% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (14 points above the panel average of 17%), and 49% usually repurchase the same brand (11 points above the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among wireless audio buyers, the most common product type is True wireless earbuds (41% of buyers). Portable speaker over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (32% of its buyers), while Over-ear headphones skews older (39% aged 25-29).

- **True wireless earbuds**: 41% of category buyers, typical price €80, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Over-ear headphones**: 35% of category buyers, typical price €180, interest rising, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Portable speaker**: 24% of category buyers, typical price €70, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.

## Trends to watch

## Implications for marketers

Market over-ears as fashion (colourways, collaborations) and earbuds on battery life and price.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 29% of buyers.
- Build the offer around performance & specs, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Online marketplaces, which carries 36% of purchases.
