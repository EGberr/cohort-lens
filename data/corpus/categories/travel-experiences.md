# Travel & Experiences: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: travel, holidays, trips, city breaks. Sector: Mobility & Travel.*

## Executive summary

56% of Gen Z consumers bought travel & experiences in the last twelve months, spending an average of €62 per month. The category is growing solidly (+11.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). TikTok is now a travel search engine for Gen Z; 'dupe destinations' (cheaper alternatives to famous places) are a growing trend.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 56%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €62
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €414
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +11.4%
- Buyers in panel: 3,350 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 59% of women bought versus 52% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 47% among 16-19 year-olds to 64% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Germany (59%) and lowest in the UK (54%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€73 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 68%, compared with 49% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (33% of buyers), followed by Instagram (27%) and Friends & family (20%). TikTok accounts for 36% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 31% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (14% vs 12%).

The main purchase channel is Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...) (47%), then Brand website / app (42%) and Instagram Shopping (5.9%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is novelty & trends (41% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (30%) and community & belonging (20%). Price is the decisive factor for 66% of buyers, 4 points above the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are cost and time off.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

28% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 25%). 4.9% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (12 points below the panel average of 17%), and 21% usually repurchase the same brand (18 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Trends to watch

- **Dupe destinations**
- **Tiktok as travel search**
- **Set-jetting**

## Implications for marketers

Optimise destination content for TikTok search and market lesser-known 'dupe' destinations on value.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 33% of buyers.
- Build the offer around novelty & trends, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Marketplaces (Amazon, Zalando...), which carries 47% of purchases.
