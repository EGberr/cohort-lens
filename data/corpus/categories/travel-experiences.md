# Travel & Experiences: Gen Z Category Report 2026

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Zentrend. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

*Also covers: travel, holidays, trips, city breaks. Sector: Entertainment & Experiences.*

## Executive summary

56% of Gen Z consumers bought travel & experiences in the last twelve months, spending an average of €62 per month. The category is growing solidly (+11.4% spend vs the 2025 wave). TikTok is now a travel search engine for Gen Z; 'dupe destinations' (cheaper alternatives to famous places) are a growing trend.

## Market snapshot

- Penetration (bought in last 12 months): 56%
- Average monthly spend among buyers: €62
- Annualised spend per Gen Z consumer: €419
- Spend growth vs 2025 wave: +11.4%
- Buyers in panel: 3,388 of 6,000 respondents

## Who buys

The category skews towards women: 60% of women bought versus 53% of men. It is an older-Gen Z category: penetration rises from 48% among 16-19 year-olds to 64% among 25-29 year-olds. By market, penetration is highest in Italy (61%) and lowest in the US (51%). Buyers in the US spend the most (€76 per month). Among high-income respondents penetration reaches 67%, compared with 49% in the low-income band.

## Path to purchase

Discovery is led by TikTok (34% of buyers), followed by Instagram (28%) and Friends & family (18%). TikTok accounts for 40% of first discoveries among 16-19 year-olds versus 30% among 25-29 year-olds, while search engines matter more for the older group (14% vs 12%).

The main purchase channel is Online marketplaces (46%), then Brand website / app (44%) and Instagram Shopping (6.2%).

## Purchase drivers and barriers

The top purchase driver is novelty & trends (42% of buyers cite it first), ahead of price / value for money (28%) and community & belonging (19%). Price is the decisive factor for 64% of buyers, in line with the panel average of 61%. The main barriers reported in open answers are cost and time off.

## Values: sustainability, dupes and loyalty

28% of buyers say they would pay more for a sustainable option (in line with the panel average of 26%). 5.0% bought a dupe or lookalike product in the last year (12 points below the panel average of 17%), and 20% usually repurchase the same brand (19 points below the panel average of 39%).

## Products Gen Z buys

Among travel & experiences buyers, the most common product type is European city break (39% of buyers). Hostel backpacking trip over-indexes with 16-19 year-olds (33% of its buyers), while Beach holiday skews older (47% aged 25-29).

- **European city break**: 39% of category buyers, typical price €350, interest rising, mostly bought via Brand website / app.
- **Hostel backpacking trip**: 32% of category buyers, typical price €250, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.
- **Beach holiday**: 28% of category buyers, typical price €600, interest stable, mostly bought via Online marketplaces.

## Trends to watch

## Implications for marketers

Optimise destination content for TikTok search and market lesser-known 'dupe' destinations on value.

- Prioritise TikTok for awareness: it is the first discovery source for 34% of buyers.
- Build the offer around novelty & trends, the most-cited driver.
- Plan conversion on Online marketplaces, which carries 46% of purchases.
