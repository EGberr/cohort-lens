The Mobility & Travel sector covers 3 categories in this panel: Travel & Experiences, Ride-hailing, Micromobility. ## Channels

## What drives purchase

Across the sector, convenience is the most common lead driver (2 of 3 categories). Travel & Experiences: TikTok is now a travel search engine for Gen Z; 'dupe destinations' (cheaper alternatives to famous places) are a growing trend. Micromobility: Shared e-bikes are replacing short ride-hail and transit trips; regulation (e-scooter bans) reshapes the market city by city.
