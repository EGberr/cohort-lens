## Overview

The Cars & Mobility sector covers 4 categories in this panel: Electric Vehicles, Used Cars, Ride-hailing, Micromobility. The fastest-growing category is Electric Vehicles (+24.5% spend), and the weakest is Ride-hailing (+2.7%).

## Channels

Main purchase channel per category: Electric Vehicles — Brand website / app (48%); Micromobility — Brand website / app (59%); Ride-hailing — Brand website / app (82%); Used Cars — Online marketplaces (51%).

## What drives purchase

Across the sector, convenience is the most common lead driver (2 of 4 categories). Electric Vehicles: Few Gen Z consumers can afford a new car, but those who buy one increasingly choose electric: leasing and small city EVs are the entry point, and YouTube range tests outrank dealer advice. Micromobility: Shared e-bikes are replacing short ride-hail and transit trips; regulation (e-scooter bans) reshapes the market city by city.
