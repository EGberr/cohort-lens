## What drives purchase

Across the sector, creator recommendation is the most common lead driver (2 of 6 categories). Fragrance: #PerfumeTok has turned fragrance into a wardrobe: Gen Z owns several scents for moods and occasions, which is why discovery sets and body mists grow faster than full-size bottles.
