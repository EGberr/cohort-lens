Gen Z is often described as disloyal, but loyalty depends on the category. The average repurchase rate is 39%; it peaks in ecosystem and habit categories such as music streaming (76%) and is lowest in trend-led categories like home decor (18%).

## Implications

In low-loyalty categories, win the next purchase through constant novelty and creator presence; in high-loyalty categories, invest in onboarding the first purchase, because it locks in years.
