## Implications

Discovery and checkout are collapsing into the same feed for low-ticket, visual categories. Brands should plan creator content as a sales channel with its own merchandising, pricing and stock planning, while high-consideration categories (tech, finance, travel) still rely on YouTube reviews and search.
