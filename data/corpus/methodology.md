# Panel Methodology

> **Synthetic data notice.** This report is part of a fictional demo corpus generated for Cohort Lens. Figures come from a simulated panel and must not be used for real decisions.

## Sample

The panel covers 6,000 respondents aged 16 to 29 across six markets (France, the UK, Germany, Spain, Italy and the US). Country split: DE 1,060, ES 876, FR 1,256, IT 785, UK 1,095, US 928. Gender: female 2,950, male 2,792, non-binary 258. Age bands: 16-19 1,706, 20-24 2,158, 25-29 2,136. Income bands: high 1,188, low 2,325, mid 2,487.

## Questions

For each of 50 product categories, respondents reported whether they bought in the last 12 months, their average monthly spend, main purchase channel, where they first discovered the product, their top purchase driver, and whether they would pay a premium for sustainability, bought a dupe, usually repurchase the same brand, and whether price was decisive.

## Definitions

- Penetration: share of all respondents who bought in the category in the last 12 months.
- Average monthly spend: mean spend among buyers, in euros.
- Annualised spend per consumer: total annual spend divided by all respondents.
- Growth: change in spend versus the 2025 wave.
- Low base: segments with fewer than 80 respondents are directional only.

## Limitations

This panel is simulated. Distributions were calibrated by hand to be plausible, and segments differ according to documented assumptions (age, gender, income and country effects). It demonstrates the Cohort Lens platform and is not market evidence.
